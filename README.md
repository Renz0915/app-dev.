# Menalist
1. **Creator:** Bruno Heller
2. **Main cast:** Simon Baker as Patrick Jane, Robin Tunney as Teresa Lisbon, with Tim Kang, Owain Yeoman and Amanda Righetti
3. **Premise:** Patrick Jane is a former fake psychic who now works as a consultant for the California Bureau of Investigation (CBI). He uses sharp observation, psychology and showmanship to help solve murders.
4. **Main storyline:** Jane is hunting Red John, the serial killer who murdered his wife and daughter. This arc runs through the series, and the later seasons shift to an FBI setting.
5. **Style:** Mostly a case-of-the-week crime drama with humor and a strong ongoing mystery.
